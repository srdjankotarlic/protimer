## What changed

### Familiar Control, more refined

Clearer small labels, consistent native-font buttons, quieter surfaces and higher-contrast action colors. Keyboard focus is visible throughout the controls. No new sidebar, navigation or removal of existing settings.

### A safer rundown workflow

- Start the rundown, then pause/resume from the same button. Previous, Next, Reset and From first are explicit actions.
- Selecting an item does not interrupt the live timer. Load prepares it; Run starts it. Replacing a running timer requires confirmation except for the deliberate Next command.
- Edit names, notes and colors, duplicate or reorder items. Changed durations apply on the next load, not halfway through a live countdown.
- Undo the last deletion. Running items must be paused before deletion.
- Fixed-height rows scroll without shrinking. The planned schedule has start, total and finish times; Show current brings the active item into view.

### Clearer phone, OBS and crew sharing

Private phone control, view-only Timer/OBS and Backstage links have separate sections. QR codes are labeled and closeable; private control QR codes cannot be sent to the audience screen. Copy failures, changed addresses, local checks and online cancellation have explicit feedback and stale-response protection.

**Local check tests the computer's own server, not reachability from a physical phone.** Test Start/Pause on the actual phone and venue network before a show. Scan a new private QR after restarting ProTimer. Online sharing remains experimental and exposes rundown notes through public views; use non-confidential content and keep a local control path available.

## Everything you already use stays

Control-only startup, the large centered preview, exact output size, independent digit scale/position, two timers, Colors/Text, Grid, Thresholds, speaker messages, frameless output, audience QR, OBS, Backstage and Companion/OSC controls remain available.

Close the old ProTimer before replacing it. Saved settings and rundown stay in your existing profile; timers do not resume running after relaunch.
