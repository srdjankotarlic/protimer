**Short description**

ProTimer is a free, open-source stage timer for Windows and Apple Silicon Macs. Send a clear countdown to a projector, confidence monitor, OBS or phone; manage a rundown with NOW/NEXT; and share view-only Timer or Backstage links by QR. No account or watermark.

## Maker comment

Hi Product Hunt — I’m Srdjan, an AV technician, camera operator and live-event video director.

I built ProTimer after seeing how often smaller productions need one simple thing: a timer the speaker can read and the operator can trust, without setting up a complex show-control system.

ProTimer 2.1 includes:

- Countdown, stopwatch and clock modes with warning colors and overtime
- A clean, frameless output for a projector, confidence monitor or OBS
- A readable rundown with START RUNDOWN, NOW/NEXT and a Backstage view
- View-only Timer and Backstage links for phones, including audience QR codes
- HTTP and OSC control; a packaged Companion module is available now while the official catalogue listing remains pending

Version 2.1 also keeps the Control preview centered, makes duration entry clearer, adds scrolling for long rundowns and improves phone synchronization.

ProTimer is free and open source. There is no account, subscription or watermark. Current builds support Windows 10/11 x64 and Apple Silicon Macs. They are not code-signed yet, so the OS may show a first-launch warning; please download only from the official GitHub release.

I’d especially value feedback from event technicians, stage managers, presenters and streamers: what would make ProTimer more useful or safer for your next show?
