# Code of Conduct

ProTimer should be a welcoming, practical place for people working in live production, software and documentation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive, including when you disagree.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Make space for people with different experience levels, languages and backgrounds.
- Protect private event information, access tokens and personal data.
- Accept correction, apologise when needed and help move the conversation forward.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualised attention, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, doxxing and publishing another person's private information are not accepted. Spam and repeatedly derailing project discussions are also out of scope.

## Reporting and enforcement

If something happens in this project that violates this code, report it privately through GitHub's [private advisory form](../../security/advisories/new). Include links or screenshots where useful, but do not post sensitive material publicly.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests or other contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as a solo-maintained project allows.

This policy is based on the principles of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
